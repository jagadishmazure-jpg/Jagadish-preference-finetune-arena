# Data card

All data is synthetic and generated by this repo. No real customers, orders or staff.

## Prompts (`data/prompts.jsonl`, from `scripts/build_dataset.py`)

| Kind | Train | Dev | Test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Policy questions | 201<!-- m:data/prompts/policy.train --> | 61<!-- m:data/prompts/policy.dev --> | 143<!-- m:data/prompts/policy.test --> |
| Unsafe requests | 54<!-- m:data/prompts/unsafe.train --> | 16<!-- m:data/prompts/unsafe.dev --> | 23<!-- m:data/prompts/unsafe.test --> |

- 13 policies of the fictional Fenwold Outdoor Supply, 4 or 5 templates each; unsafe kinds: another
  customer's order, rule bypass, staff secrets, out-of-scope help (3 templates each).
- Each template gets up to 8 variants from polite prefixes and suffixes; duplicates removed.
- Split by a hash of the template: no wording family is in two splits.

## Candidates (`data/candidates.jsonl`)

1,992 answers (4 per prompt), qwen2.5-0.5b and qwen2.5-1.5b instruct, Q4_K_M GGUF, llama.cpp on CPU,
up to 120 new tokens, seeds recorded per answer.

## Pairs

- Best vs worst with a rubric gap of 0.2 or more: train 251<!-- m:data/pairs/train -->, dev
  76<!-- m:data/pairs/dev -->, test 162<!-- m:data/pairs/test -->.
- Chosen answers are shorter: median 15<!-- m:data/median_words_chosen --> words against
  88<!-- m:data/median_words_rejected -->; the chosen answer is the longer one in only
  13.9%<!-- m:data/chosen_longer/rate --> of train pairs. A model can learn "shorter is better" from this
  data, which is why length rules are reported as baselines.
- All clear pairs (gap 0.1 or more) for reward models: train 1,303<!-- m:reward/_train_pairs -->, test
  857<!-- m:reward/_test_pairs -->.

## Measured outputs

`data/generations.jsonl` (6 variants x 243 dev and test prompts), `data/judge.jsonl` (1,320 judge
replies), `data/train_log.jsonl`, `data/tune_manifest.json`, `data/emb/embeddings.npz` (bge-small,
384 numbers per text, float16). SHA-256 of each is in `data/manifest.json`, checked in CI.

## Known biases

Templated English only; one store; policies are short and single-fact. The careful style comes from
the same model as the judge (self-preference is reported). Labels reflect the rubric, not people.

Candidate and tuned answers are kept exactly as the models wrote them. Small models invent details,
and some answers name real stores or carriers that have nothing to do with this project; those files
are excluded from the brand check for that reason only.
